Public Health Transformation

Strengthening Primary Care Without Replacing It

Addressing core structural bottlenecks in rural delivery through an integrated, continuous, and accountable digital health mesh.

Assisted Teleconsultation

Patients lack smartphones and digital literacy. Frontline ASHA and ANM workers use guided tablets to connect patients with district hospital doctors and specialists in seconds.

  • Bandwidth-adaptive audio/video (operates on 2G/EDGE)
  • Digital stethoscope and vitals input
  • Instant vernacular prescription generation

Digital Triage & Escalation

Standardized clinical decision support identifies red flags in maternal, infant, and acute cases. Automatic direct escalation to 108 emergency medical dispatch.

  • Validated color-coded triaging (Green / Yellow / Red)
  • Maternal pre-eclampsia & hemorrhage protocols
  • Automated SMS/IVR alert to District Chief Medical Officer

Longitudinal ABHA Records

Eliminates lost paper slips. When a patient moves from a Sub-Centre to a PHC and then to a District Hospital, all vitals, lab reports, and medications remain unified under their ABHA ID.

  • ABDM / Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission aligned
  • Biometric or OTP-based consent architecture
  • Offline cryptographic record caching

Real-Time Medicine & Lab Visibility

Prevents patients travelling 30 km only to find essential medicines out of stock. Live inventory tracking across Sub-Centres, PHCs, and CHCs with automatic reorder triggers.

  • Essential Drug List (EDL) compliance monitor
  • Diagnostic test availability by facility
  • Community stockout transparency

Closed-Loop Referral Tracking

Ensures no patient falls through the cracks. Tracks referred patients until consultation is done, with post-discharge home visit reminders sent straight to their local ASHA worker.

  • Two-way facility referral handshake
  • Automated reminder calls in regional dialects
  • High-risk pregnancy delivery tracking

Multilingual & Voice Assistance

Breaks literacy barriers. Frontline workers and villagers receive voice-guided health summaries, dosage reminders, and emergency instructions in local languages.

  • Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil support
  • Audio instructions for low-literacy patients
  • Pictorial dosage schedule charts
